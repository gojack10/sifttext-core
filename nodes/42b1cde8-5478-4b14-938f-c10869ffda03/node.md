Record a bounded question for each promising approach when the route to an outcome is unknown.

Investigate within the user's authority and preserve what the evidence establishes.

Discard a rejected approach with its reason, keeping useful findings available to continuing work.

A later agent can inspect those grounds before repeating an earlier attempt.

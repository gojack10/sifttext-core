A person brings an understanding of the situation and associations worth investigating.

The tree records those ideas as focused questions with links to their grounds.

The current agent applies its available reasoning to the relevant nodes, and the person can inspect or redirect the work.

Saved understanding lets another conversation continue from the same grounds.

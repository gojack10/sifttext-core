## Choose an owner
Read the source and nearby possible owners before deciding where new understanding belongs.

Keep a bounded observation with the explanation it supports.

Complete the original question with its result while preserving what was known at its beginning.

Give an independently meaningful development its own node beneath the work it extends.

Keep routine bookkeeping within its existing owner.

## Explain the development
Let each local outline expose the meaningful branches a reader can choose.

Keep parallel investigations as siblings when neither develops the other.

Add a deeper owner only for a real subproblem or synthesis.

Use references for shared evidence without duplicating whole answers.

Retain useful findings beneath a rejected approach when that is their origin.

## Reorganize honestly
Read both parents and the moved node before changing parentage.

Keep its UUID and source content, recording a meaningful former placement.

Distinguish a present reorganization from evidence about how the work originally developed.

Update affected navigation within the approved change.

## Respect the tree's audience
User-instruction trees contain current procedures and useful teaching examples.

Maintain current user instructions at stable IDs and recover earlier editions through Git.

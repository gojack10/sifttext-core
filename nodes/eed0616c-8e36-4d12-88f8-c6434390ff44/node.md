Applies to generated interfaces, documents, spreadsheets, slides, and similar artifacts.

1. Content
Include what was asked for. Don't add introductions, summaries, instructions, explanations, disclaimers, process notes, or extra sections unless explicitly requested. “Might be helpful” is not a reason to include them.

2. Language
Use plain, direct wording. No promotional language, inflated descriptions, repeated explanations, or commentary about the artifact's quality. State the content without announcing, praising, or explaining its presentation.

3. Styling
Monochrome is preferred. Use readable typography, ordinary spacing, and light formatting. No decorative symbols, emojis, badges, gradients, shadows, oversized headings, or boxes around everything unless requested. Don't confuse minimal styling with tiny fonts or cramped layouts.

Explicit task requirements and supplied style instructions take precedence.

## Represent knowledge

```markdown
## Observed conditions
## Inference and response
```

Reusable instructions state their purpose and procedure directly, while their development records belong outside the user-instruction tree.

Read [[Grounded Node Examples|7a5ea88c-daf8-4bab-ab9e-f1a1a789a17a]] when authoring or reviewing knowledge records.

Use [[Stable Navigation Fields|5a7e7c35-ac0c-4590-8e1b-a208fa71febe]] when authoring the name and micro-summary, keeping navigation separate from current results and disposition.

## Observed conditions
Identify what was supplied or examined with enough context for a first-time reader.

Name the source actor and relevant evidence version when they affect interpretation.

Reference an existing evidence owner with a brief explanation of its contribution. Name contributing children or parents in the body; add see_also with a reason for contributing non-child nodes.

Preserve consequential wording with its actual role as a request or claim.

Keep quotations exact within marked excerpt boundaries and retain a recoverable original.

Attribute recollections to their speaker and leave missing sources explicit.

## Inference and response
Identify the person or agent who made the judgment, and explain what evidence supports it.

State whether the response was proposed, selected, attempted or observed complete.

Preserve uncertainty without inventing a rationale to fill the section.

Keep hypotheses and qualifications within the node’s actual topic. A contrast such as “A does not prove B” belongs when that relationship matters to the question; do not import an unasked restriction as inherited guidance.

Treat agent-authored prose as such unless source evidence establishes human authorship.

Human approval authorizes its stated scope.

Ordinary delegated methods remain revisable.

Repeated summaries of one observation retain one evidence lineage.

## Develop and complete

Use [[Knowledge Layout|b6107909-a33b-4c63-ab0d-351ba1a1b672]] for native status semantics and required reasons.

Choose status from the node's own completion condition.

Preserve prior status reasons before clearing them.

A captured report can resolve while its explanation remains uncertain.

Keep required unfinished work open.

## Save
Return the grounded candidate to its invoking author or reviewer.

Save through [[SiftText Commit|c72d552d-3499-445b-a8d5-05d0ff7824f2]] within the actual approval.

A formatting edit must retain evidence needed to understand the claim.

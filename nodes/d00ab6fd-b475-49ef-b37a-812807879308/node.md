Use [[Draft From Originating Conditions|d96c84a8-ce99-4474-94ef-7ae1d1db4a1b]] to form a source-grounded candidate for the user's intended change.

Explain a genuinely unresolved choice before asking for it.

Use [[Present the Reviewable Delta|4036d3c5-e596-4a9c-b432-1c4ccc3162b4]] to show coherent parts of the proposed change.

Review corrections through [[Forcing Pass|da24ff95-9d07-4e15-9571-9313d6b90f94]].

Ask approval before saving.

Skip that step only when the user explicitly preapproves the change.

## Purpose

Use the name and micro-summary to identify the unit and explain why a reader would open it. They describe its stable subject and contribution; the node’s content supplies the actual observations, reasoning and outcome.

## Name

Name the bounded question, work, finding or method. Prefer an identity that remains accurate as that unit’s answer or status develops, rather than renaming it after each new result. A later independent development should become visible in the tree instead of silently changing which unit the title denotes.

## Micro-summary

Expand the title with the stable contribution or question available for inspection. Be specific enough to distinguish this node from its neighbors without turning the field into its current answer, progress, winner or next action. Avoid a generic invitation to read more that would fit every node.

## Changes and continuation

Keep the micro-summary when the unit’s purpose remains the same after a result or status change. Correct a field that misstates that purpose, and reconsider the boundary when the content has become a different subject. Status and its required reasons carry disposition; the content explains what happened and any next action supported by the current evidence.

Return these fields to the procedure authoring or reviewing the complete node.

A tree without links is just a hierarchy. Links are what make it a map.

Consider a tree about expanding your business into Europe. You have a "Regulatory Requirements" node and a "Hiring Plan" node in different branches. Your intuition tells you these are connected: compliance staffing depends on which regulations apply. You create a link.

That link has consequences.

A language model working on your hiring plan in isolation might not know to check regulatory requirements. It would produce a staffing plan that ignores compliance constraints. But with the link, the model traverses the connection, loads the regulatory context, and factors it into the plan. Your intuition became the model's map. A dependency you noticed because of your experience is now a navigable path in the structure.

It works in both directions. A language model working through your tree might discover a dependency you missed. It reads a node about market research and notices an assumption that depends on pricing decisions three branches away. It creates the link. Now your tree captures a connection that neither of you would have maintained in your head.

This is the human-LLM partnership at its most concrete:
- **Humans surface connections through intuition.** You notice relationships because you have lived experience, gut feel, and the ability to pattern-match across your entire mesh of knowledge.
- **Language models traverse connections at depth.** They follow links, load context, and reason about implications with a thoroughness you cannot sustain across dozens of nodes.

Over time, the tree becomes richer from both sides. Every link you create is a permanent piece of navigational infrastructure. Every link a language model creates is a dependency you might not have noticed. The structure gets smarter the more both sides use it.

This is why linking is the highest-leverage activity in SiftText. Creating a node captures a piece of thinking. Linking two nodes captures a relationship between pieces. Relationships are where insight lives. A tree with many linked nodes is not just organized information. It is a map of how ideas connect, and every connection is a path that a language model can traverse to bring you depth.

## Observed conditions

The parent preserves the preceding local Dialogue procedure, including source reconstruction, Unix deltas and ordinary-chat approval. The source-origin drafting method adds a per-node check: execution results alone do not explain the exchange or observation that made a development meaningful. This local adaptation retains the existing presentation and permission contract without requiring a hosted service or private instruction tree.

## Inference and response

### Entry and source recovery

Receive the requested result, permitted source range, affected knowledge and actual grant through local SiftText Commit. Read its required pre-write entries and affected owners; reuse unchanged reads. Proposal-only work ends at review. Approval covers its stated delta; a standing grant covers only its scope. Missing evidence or authority stops dependent work, not unrelated authorized work.

For a retrospective, cover the meaningful developments across the supplied source range. For a focused change, cover its affected subject and context. Preserve distinct discoveries, attempted alternatives, failures, changing assumptions and unfinished commitments without inventing a node for each command.

For each affected node, recover what introduced its subject: the initiating human exchange, existing recorded condition, tool observation or agent discovery. Preserve consequential source words, the actor's stated reason, uncertainty, response and later corrections locally, or identify the existing evidence owner and explain its substantive contribution. A top-level quote or a source path alone does not supply a child's otherwise missing origin. A tool-origin finding needs its actual observation, not an invented human quote.

Quote selected source words exactly, marking omissions and editorial clarification. Attribute questions, reports, proposals, selections and approvals to their actual actors. A peer assignment is not a human requirement; recollection is not independent observation. Do not reconstruct missing words or reasons. Evidence-only work retains source actors' interpretations and rationale without adding the current author's diagnosis or repair selection.

### Form the changed understanding

Use the existing meaningful owner. Complete its bounded undertaking without rewriting what was known at its origin. Give an independently changing discovery, correction, attempted alternative or successor its own owner beneath the work it develops. Alternatives belong under their shared question rather than under a competing answer. Chronology alone does not establish causality.

Write new units with Observed conditions followed by Inference and response, explaining why the attributed response follows. Preserve meaningful earlier judgments and complete procedures. A substantive instruction revision supplies a complete successor or coherent selected branch set; its entry makes current execution explicit without requiring historical-amendment replay. A new observation does not automatically override a human constraint or invalidate an earlier judgment.

Identify materially affected current consumers, including unchanged passages and owners not initially proposed for editing. Include supported in-scope repairs in the delta. Leave genuinely unresolved meanings and out-of-scope repairs explicit; do not turn this into unrelated forest cleanup.

### Present the actual delta

Render affected areas directly in ordinary assistant chat, in a fenced text code block using Unix branches. Show enough unchanged context for orientation, not the entire forest. Use operation then effective status, for example `[!][~] Existing Node`.

Operations: `[+]` create, `[!]` field/status change, `[→]` move, `[←]` reference change, `[-]` deletion. Statuses: `[ ]` not examined, `[~]` in progress, `[✓]` resolved, `[x]` discarded, `[=]` deferred, `[/]` stuck. Unchanged context has only its status. Model normalization's actual activation and ancestor reopening rather than displaying cosmetic prior statuses.

For each changed node show its actual ID or visibly temporary new label, parent, name, stable-purpose micro-summary, effective status and exact changed fields. Alongside it, show the exact changed content using contextual diffs or complete replacement bodies. New methods must be complete, not amendment fragments. Label any abridged reading view and identify omissions; it is not approval of unseen content.

For each actual reference operation identify source field, real target and relationship. Native indexed references require `see_also` metadata with an existing UUID and nonblank reason; narrative citations do not create graph edges. Direct parent/child structure is not a redundant reference. Resolve new IDs from actual creation before saving dependent references. A move retains identity; discarding is not deletion.

A compact fictional example of the notation follows. It is not a project instruction or a source of real IDs:

```text
[~] Supplier Investigation
├── [+][✓] Late Deliveries Observed
│   Add micro: Records delivery evidence that challenges the prior choice.
│   Add cryst: The reviewed shipment records establish the stated delays.
└── [+][ ] Trial Another Supplier
    Add micro: Tests the proposed replacement before broader adoption.
    Add cryst: No trial exists yet; verify delivery and quality before resolving.
```

### Review, approval and return

Use the parent's sibling Forcing Pass and its selected current method before presentation or execution. For instruction changes also apply local Canopy. Recheck changed portions after molding; unchanged reviewed work needs no repeated full pass.

These changes must appear in chat rather than only in a file link, summary, or approval-button label. When approval is required, ask through ordinary chat and wait for the human to explicitly authorize the stated delta there. Do not use harness question, decision, or approval widgets for SiftText Commit. Under an explicit standing write grant, proceed with the reviewed in-scope delta without asking again.

Suspend only the affected work when a new human-owned choice arises, an override is unclear, authority is missing or the human stops it. Preserve unrelated constraints and work. Return the reviewed, authorized delta and its source/permission basis to Persistence. Its native worktree and checked-publication mechanics remain unchanged. A displayed proposal is not a saved change or demonstrated future-agent behavior.

## Recover the source
Receive the requested change through [[SiftText Commit|c72d552d-3499-445b-a8d5-05d0ff7824f2]] and follow its source limits.

Recover what introduced each affected subject before summarizing its outcome.

Preserve the actual source wording and any reason the actor supplied when they matter to the resulting knowledge.

Include later corrections that change how the original words should be understood.

For tool-origin discoveries, record the observed trigger without fabricating a human quotation.

Identify an unavailable speaker or source explicitly.

## Form the change
Complete each bounded record with what happened while preserving its original conditions.

Keep source wording distinguishable from the author's interpretation.

Apply supported corrections to the affected current fields.

For an evidence-only assignment, preserve the source actors' reasons while omitting new diagnoses by the current author.

## Prepare review
Use [[Associate External Artifact Directories|de7cea55-c93d-48db-a4f6-58368bf6c36f]] for directories materially used by affected nodes.

Present the exact candidate through [[Present the Reviewable Delta|4036d3c5-e596-4a9c-b432-1c4ccc3162b4]].

Identify an abridged display as such without substituting it for the stored candidate.

Send the complete candidate and source basis to [[Review Origin and Response Coverage|ab82fdf1-6a71-4e09-bffe-ed6446eadc9a]].

Saving proceeds through [[Save and Restore Continuation|a609e77c-8164-40d2-b391-e675194fd2ad]] only under Commit's approval boundary.

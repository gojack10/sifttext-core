Decide whether the new observation contradicts the conclusion, changes when it applies, or leaves it unchanged.

Record a substantive challenge beneath the understanding it challenges, with its own evidence.

Preserve the earlier judgment, and explain why the supported current disposition changes or stays the same.

Identify the dependent action that must wait when a challenge remains unresolved.

Correct materially affected consumers within scope.

Keep useful findings reachable even when their original approach was rejected.

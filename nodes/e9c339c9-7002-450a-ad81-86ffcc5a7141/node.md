## Observed conditions

Two reviewed local Commit runs left affected instructions inconsistent.
The first received the main procedure and its three pre-write branches
but did not receive the finishing-stage bodies. The later run received
those bodies, including their contradiction checks, yet recorded another
fact without repairing the affected current instruction.

Both results checked the addition more successfully than the resulting
understanding. This motivates bringing the finishing contract into the
main procedure and checking what the affected knowledge now supports.
It does not establish that this revision improves agent behavior.

## Inference and response

### Purpose, reading and authority

A Commit preserves how understanding developed and leaves its affected
current uses coherent. A discovery, correction or decision is not
integrated merely because its words have been saved.

Read this complete procedure and the parent's children named Dialogue,
Forcing Pass and Persistence before writing. Locate their files through
the parent's children.order and children directory. Reuse unchanged reads;
reload after context loss, a relevant revision or a concrete conflict.
Read the affected knowledge and applicable local project instructions.
An unavailable required input stops the work that depends on it.

Dialogue owns source reconstruction and exact proposal presentation.
Forcing Pass owns proposal review. Persistence and Worktree Publication
own local file, baseline, native-check and publication mechanics.
The finishing section below owns reconciliation and verification for
this route; the earlier separate bodies are historical references.

The human-facing agent proposes and checks the change. A bounded worker
stays within its assignment and returns a private candidate to its
assigning agent. Approval authorizes its stated delta; a standing grant
covers its actual scope without repeated approval. Discussion or silence
does not grant permission. Proposal-only work ends at review.

### Form the changed understanding

Treat the chat as one unit for end-of-chat capture.
For that capture, carry ordinary mid-chat requests to remember or save something without interrupting the ongoing task for save approval or asking “now or later?”
An explicit instruction to commit now overrides this end-of-chat timing.
Otherwise, when the human indicates the chat is ending, or its conclusion is otherwise clear, prepare the delta and follow the existing approval or standing-grant boundary.

For a context handoff before chat-end, follow the Read procedure's handoff section.

Use Dialogue to recover the relevant source and prepare the exact delta.
For a retrospective, cover the meaningful developments across the
requested source range; for a focused correction, cover its affected
subject. Preserve consequential human wording, stated reasons, later
corrections, uncertainty and the distinction between observation and
interpretation.

Identify what the new evidence changes about an existing answer,
assumption, method or open question. Inspect the current owners and
instructions that materially depend on it, including affected nodes
that were not initially proposed for editing. Include supported
in-scope changes in the delta. Unrelated cleanup is outside this pass.

Preserve the earlier unit's originating conditions and reasoning.
Complete its bounded undertaking with what happened and its supported
disposition. Give a substantive correction, discovery or replacement
method its own meaningful development. Write new units with Observed
conditions and Inference and response, explaining why the response
follows rather than merely supplying headings.

For a changed procedure, preserve the complete earlier procedure,
provide a complete successor or coherent selected branch set, and make
the current execution selection explicit. Update concise dispositions
and affected current uses; do not rewrite the past as though the later
evidence was known then. A newer observation does not automatically
invalidate an earlier judgment or override a human requirement.

A finding may leave existing guidance correct. Preserve that result
without a ceremonial rewrite. An unresolved conflict remains explicit,
with the affected action and next investigation or human decision.

Run Forcing Pass before presentation or execution. For instruction
changes, apply the bundled Canopy procedure. Present the exact delta
when approval is required. Recheck changed portions after molding;
unchanged reviewed work needs no repeated full pass.

### Save and finish the affected understanding

Use Persistence to apply only authorized edits in the private knowledge
worktree. Preserve concurrent work, fixed-baseline checks, supported
status transitions and failed drafts. Inspect uncertain operations
before retrying them.

Before candidate handoff or checked publication, inspect the resulting
affected knowledge, not just the diff or the newly added sentence:

- Read back promised fields, structure, references and effective
  statuses against the authorized delta.
- Start from the entry a later reader would use for the affected
  question. Follow its current selection and relevant owners.
- State the supported current answer or next action and identify the
  passages that supply it. Check those passages against the source
  evidence and actual authority, including affected unchanged wording.
- Confirm that the earlier understanding remains inspectable, while
  obsolete instructions do not still compete as current guidance.

For example, recording that a helper does not support one environment
does not repair an entry that still directs every environment to use
that helper. Preserve the discovery and its grounds, then make the
supported current procedure reachable from that entry.

If the reader must know this conversation, combine incompatible
instructions or supply a missing decision, the affected repair is
unfinished. Repair within the grant and recheck the changed parts.
If evidence or authority is missing, report that exact gap and suspend
dependent work; continue independent authorized work.

Repeat applicable normalization and complete native candidate checks
after repairs. Native checks establish their declared structural and
storage properties, not semantic correctness.

The bounded worker returns its checked private candidate and findings.
An authorized publisher follows Worktree Publication's checked
submission and recovery procedure, then verifies the landed result and
receipt identities. Refresh and recheck affected meaning if integration
changes the reviewed content. Neither a private commit nor a successful
submission substitutes for the semantic check above.

### Return and stop

Report the actual changes, revision and publication boundary, the
current answer or continuation checked, its supporting node passages,
and any remaining gap. A no-change reconciliation is valid when the
inspection supports it.

Call the requested Commit complete only when its promised operations
and affected-understanding checks pass. Distinguish completed evidence
capture from an unresolved subject or repair.

The human may pause or reshape the route; preserve the scope of the
override and unrelated constraints. Missing authority, unsafe paths,
failed checks or unavailable evidence stop dependent work with an
explicit restart condition.

This walkthrough checks the saved representation. Improved behavior
by a fresh agent requires separate evidence; no model launch, evaluation
run or remote push is authorized by this procedure.

Your local knowledge files and Git history remain available between sessions. Core does not keep an assistant running or remember unsaved conversations. A returning human or external agent must read the relevant current nodes and recover the actual open question, evidence and next authorized action.

You can keep growing the tree, find earlier work with local retrieval, refine bodies and explicit references, or start a new problem. External agents can propose changes under your permission; native validation checks structure, not the truth of their recommendations.

Save approved contributions through Worktree Publication. Successful `submit` leaves the normal home and retrieval current and removes the completed draft. Read from that home and allocate a new private worktree for the next approved edit. After an interrupted save, preserve its original arguments and follow Worktree Publication's retry/recovery procedure before claiming completion. Existing homes are not silently reseeded by an upgrade. Return actual source/check/publication evidence to the original task and keep unfinished questions open rather than claiming that a stored plan proves success.

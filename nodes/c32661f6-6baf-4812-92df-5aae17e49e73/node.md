Preserve what was considered or tried, why it was rejected and under which conditions. Otherwise a fresh person or agent can waste work rediscovering the same boundary.

A distinct rejected approach gets a discarded node, a nonblank `discard_reason`, and its rationale/evidence in the body. Distinguish a tried failure from rejection through reasoning; inconclusive or untried work is not a measured failure. A small qualification without an independent lifecycle can stay in the owning body.

For example, a rejected whole-report buffering approach can retain its actual measured memory result and the target it exceeded. A rejected hosted-processing approach can state that it violated an offline constraint without pretending it was trialled. Keep reusable measurements under a non-discarded owner and reference them rather than discarding useful evidence with the approach.

Read rejected approaches before proposing them again. New conditions or explicit human direction can justify reopening the question. Preserve history rather than overwrite the earlier rationale as if it never existed.

## Current purpose
Produce minimum viable human-readable text that preserves the meaning needed for the reader's decision. Apply to authored instructions and knowledge text. [[Canopy|957aa569-75be-4871-b376-6b1943cc4db4]] governs instruction design; this pass governs expression.

## Working pass
State the actual observation, judgment or action directly. Keep the relevant actor, conditions, evidence and source. Separate supplied facts, interpretations and decisions where that distinction changes the meaning.

Remove repeated caveats, procedural self-commentary, generic future exceptions and duplicated conclusions. Express a material limitation as the concrete remaining uncertainty or work. Use normal sentences; shorter text is useful only while the important meaning remains recoverable.

Preserve quoted human wording character for character inside the selected excerpt. Mark omissions and put an interpretation outside the quote. A typographical correction to agent-authored explanation is different from rewriting the human's source.

When shortening would require guessing, inspect the relevant source or preserve the uncertainty. Ask the human only for a genuinely missing decision or meaning needed for the task. Do not weaken an explicit boundary merely to shorten the text.

## Examples
Agent report: “The launch test passed. Recovery after a controller restart remains untested.”

Grounded choice: “Two builds sharing one output directory produced inconsistent files. The team selected serial execution for that arrangement.”

Attributed interpretation: “Jack asked us to reduce unnecessary storage. The agent selected 500 MB as its initial target.”

These are fictional examples of wording. Preserve real conditions and evidence in an actual node.

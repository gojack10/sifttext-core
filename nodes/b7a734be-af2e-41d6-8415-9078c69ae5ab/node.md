Read the owner's purpose and current disposition before selecting a branch from its immediate children.

Open relevant bodies and descend when the question exposes a material gap.

Include useful resolved findings and rejected approaches when their evidence matters.

Inspect the actual returned outline before treating its depth as bounded.

Preserve child order and disclose omitted pages of a wide branch.

Batch independent relevant reads when the interface supports it.

Read conditional instructions when their stated trigger applies.

Reuse unchanged instruction bodies until context loss or a relevant revision requires refreshing them.

Return enough grounded context to continue the assigned task.
